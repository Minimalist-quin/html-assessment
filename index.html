My Biography

I’m Grace Akpoiroro, a product designer with over six years of experience designing digital products across fintech, B2B SaaS, and e-commerce. Over the years, I’ve worked on products ranging from financial infrastructure and payment solutions to business management platforms. I’ve also become increasingly interested in how products are built beyond the design layer, which led me to start exploring AI-assisted development and agentic tools.

I joined AltSchool’s School of Engineering to build a stronger technical foundation, deepen my understanding of software development, and become better equipped to turn ideas and designs into functional products.

Why AltSchool Africa

  • I chose AltSchool because I wanted to go beyond designing digital products and develop the technical skills needed to build them. As a product designer, I work closely with engineers, and I want to have a stronger understanding of the technology behind the products I design.
  • I also chose AltSchool because it offers an accredited diploma program, which gives me the opportunity to gain formal recognition for the skills and knowledge I develop throughout the program. I believe the School of Engineering will help me build a strong foundation in software development, improve my collaboration with engineering teams, and become a more well-rounded product builder.

My Goals

  1. Build strong software engineering skills by developing a solid foundation in programming, software development, databases, APIs, and system design.
  2. Become a better product builder by combining my product design experience with engineering skills so I can turn ideas and designs into functional, scalable products.
  3. Build real-world products and grow professionally by applying what I learn to practical projects, contribute effectively to engineering teams, and grow into a well-rounded product-focused engineer.
